Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Masjid Ar-Raheem is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit rodeomasjid.org, contact us, subscribe to updates, register for programs, make donations, or receive communications from us.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Program registration details, volunteer interests, donation-related information, and message preferences.
  • Communications you send to us by email, forms, phone, text message, or other channels.

We may also collect basic website usage information, such as browser type, pages visited, referring links, and similar technical information used to maintain and improve our website.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide community updates, event notices, prayer schedule information, program information, and administrative communications.
  • Respond to questions, requests, registrations, and support needs.
  • Manage donations, records, services, and mosque operations.
  • Improve our website, communications, programs, and community services.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, safety, and security obligations.

Text Messaging and Mobile Information

If you provide your mobile phone number and opt in to receive text messages from Masjid Ar-Raheem, we may send SMS messages related to mosque announcements, prayer or event updates, program reminders, community alerts, donation reminders, and related administrative information.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact us at board@rodeomasjid.org.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information only when needed to operate our website, manage programs, process donations, send communications, comply with law, protect rights and safety, or work with trusted service providers who support our operations.

Service providers are expected to use information only for the services they provide to Masjid Ar-Raheem and to protect it appropriately.

Cookies and Third-Party Services

Our website may use cookies, embedded content, analytics, email subscription tools, donation tools, maps, social media features, or other third-party services. These providers may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. However, no website, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may request that we update or remove your contact information, unsubscribe from email updates using unsubscribe links where available, or opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP.

Children's Privacy

Our website and communications are intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate parental or guardian consent.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our communications, contact us at board@rodeomasjid.org.
